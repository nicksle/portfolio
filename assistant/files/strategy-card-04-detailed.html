Card 04: Integrating Education & Guidance

We designed seamless educational moments throughout the experience—contextual tooltips, clear error states, visible incentives, and progressive feature introduction—to ensure users never feel lost in the app.

Journey map with highlighted confusion moments
↓ Annotations (Position: After Image)
01 Page Entry Points
Users navigating to new screens couldn't immediately identify page purpose or available actions.
02 Feature Discovery
Users didn't understand circle mechanics evaluation criteria or how to assess which circle to join.
03 Blocking Moments
When hitting restrictions users received generic alerts without understanding why or what to do next.
04 Critical Decision Points
Before sensitive information requests users needed context about why data was required and how it would be used.
05 Progress Uncertainty
Users couldn't tell how far along they were in onboarding or what steps remained to access key features.
06 Hidden Value
Rewards achievements and incentives existed but were never surfaced missing opportunities to motivate completion.
SLIDE 02 Exploring Education Options
Image
education-pattern-library.svg
8 different education approaches compared
↓ Annotations (Position: After Image)
01 Contextual Tooltips
Small info icons revealing explanations on tap. Minimal visual footprint but requires user action to discover.
02 First-Time Modals
Full-screen or overlay explanations on first visit. Ensures visibility but can feel interruptive.
03 Progressive Walkthroughs
Step-by-step guided tours highlighting key features. Comprehensive but forces linear learning path.
04 Inline Explanations
Educational content embedded directly in interface. Always visible but takes up screen space.
05 Video Tutorials
Short explainer videos for complex features. Engaging but requires user commitment and data bandwidth.
06 Coach Marks
Animated pointers drawing attention to UI elements. Eye-catching but can feel gimmicky if overused.

↓ Annotations (Position: After All Images)
01 Sticky vs Dismissible Carousel
Current carousel scrolls away with content causing users to lose track. Explored sticky positioning to maintain visibility.
02 Integrated Task Cards
Instead of separate onboarding carousel embed next steps directly within relevant pages (e.g. "Complete KYC to join circles" on circle detail).
03 Progress Indicators
Add persistent progress tracking showing completion status without dominating screen real estate.
04 Contextual Prompts
Trigger guidance at moment of need (e.g. explain KYC when user attempts to join circle) rather than frontloading all information.
05 Narrative Cohesion
Connect onboarding steps with clear explanations of how each unlocks next capability creating momentum rather than obligation.
06 Reward Visibility
Surface incentives and achievements throughout the journey to provide clear motivation for completing steps.
