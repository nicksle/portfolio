Card 02: Streamlining Sign-Up Flow

Through competitive analysis and rapid prototyping, we established best practices for sign-up and tested new flow variations to reduce friction and improve completion rates.

📊 4 Slides | 🎯 14 Total Annotations | 🖼️ ~10 Images Expected
SLIDE 01 Exploring Sign Up Options
Image
signup-methods-comparison.svg
Side-by-side comparison of 4 sign-up approaches
↓ Annotations (Position: After Image)
01 Email + Password (Traditional)
Standard account creation with email verification. Most familiar pattern but adds extra verification step
02 Phone Number First
SMS-based verification with immediate access. Fast but raises privacy concerns and creates dependency on phone service
03 Social Sign-In (Google/Apple)
One-tap authentication using existing accounts. Fastest entry but some users distrust data sharing
04 Magic Link
Passwordless email link authentication. Removes password friction but requires email access during each login
SLIDE 02 Streamlining Email Sign Up
Image

Original email sign-up flow
Image

Streamlined email sign-up with clear verification
↓ Annotations (Position: After All Images)
01 Clear Verification States
Email verification now shows which email is being verified with clear status indicators. Users cannot proceed until verified.
02 Distinction between Sign Up and Login
Visual hierarchy and clear labels help users distinguish flows. Added "Don't have an account?" redirection to prevent confusion.
03 Implementing Best Practices
Broke single-page form into logical steps. Progressive disclosure improved completion while maintaining necessary information gathering.
SLIDE 03 Removing Redundancy
Image
redundant-steps-audit.svg
Flow diagram showing unnecessary steps marked for removal
Image
streamlined-flow.svg
Final streamlined flow after removals
↓ Annotations (Position: After All Images)
01 Phone Verification Removed
Not technically required and caused CloudFlare timeout bugs. Eliminated entire step and associated errors
02 Profile Picture Deferred
Moved from sign-up to post-onboarding. Low engagement during sign-up better suited for profile completion
03 Job Title Removed
Non-essential information that added friction. Can be collected later if needed for specific features
04 Form Field Optimization
Fixed paste detection bug for email input improved validation feedback
