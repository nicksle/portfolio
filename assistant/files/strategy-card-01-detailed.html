Card 01: Understanding Value & Building Trust

We conducted user testing to identify which value propositions resonated most with our target users and how we could effectively communicate trust and security throughout the onboarding experience.

SLIDE 01 The Challenge
Image
kyc-trust-barrier.svg
Shows user hesitation at KYC screen
↓ Annotations (Position: After Image)
01 Asking Before Establishing Credibility
Users abandoned during KYC because we were asking for sensitive information before establishing credibility. They had no context for why we needed their data.
02 No Assurance of Data Protection
No visible security indicators or privacy assurances at critical input moments left users feeling uncertain about data handling.
SLIDE 02 Value Prop Brainstorming
Image
value-prop-matrix.svg
Matrix of different value propositions
↓ Annotations (Position: After Image)
01 Quick Cash Access
"Access emergency funds when you need them" - Emphasizes immediate financial relief and speed
02 Community Savings
"Save together with friends and family" - Focuses on social connection and collective wealth building
03 Financial Goals
"Achieve your financial goals faster" - Highlights specific outcomes like vacations, emergency funds, or down payments
04 No Credit Check
"Get money without credit checks" - Addresses financial inclusion and removes traditional barriers
05 Flexible Contributions
"Save at your own pace" - Emphasizes control and lack of rigid requirements
06 Social Accountability
"Stay motivated with your circle" - Leverages peer support and commitment
SLIDE 03 Trust Signal Brainstorm
Image
trust-signals-inventory.svg
Catalog of trust-building elements
↓ Annotations (Position: After Image)
01 Encryption Indicators
Bank-level encryption badge with explanation of data security measures
02 Compliance Badges
Display regulatory compliance (FDIC insured, SOC 2 certified) near sensitive input fields
03 Privacy Policy Links
Clear, accessible privacy policy with plain language explanations
04 Security Certifications
Third-party security certifications and audit results
SLIDE 04 Testing and Implementation
Image

Image

↓ Annotations (Position: After All Images)
01 Preference Tests
A/B tested value proposition messaging with 25 users. "Quick Cash Access" showed highest engagement and conversion intent.
02 User Interviews
12 users discussed trust concerns. Encryption indicators and compliance badges most frequently mentioned as confidence builders.
03 Finding: Emergency Funds as Primary Driver
"Access to emergency funds" resonated as the primary value driver. Pairing with encryption indicators increased user confidence during testing.
04 Integration into Prototypes
Validated messaging integrated into wireframes and prototypes, allowing subsequent testing to focus on flow patterns rather than re-validating messaging.
